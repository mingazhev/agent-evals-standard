# Reproducible Source Evidence Catalog

- Status: unpublished 0.1.0 publication candidate
- Version: 0.1.0
- Retrieval date for all mutable web locators: 2026-08-01

Sources are informative. They explain the evidence basis and limitations of
requirements; they do not import external benchmark rules. The machine-readable
[`source-evidence-manifest.json`](source-evidence-manifest.json) records support
strength, structured empirical observations, represented populations,
target-population validation, contrary evidence, projection-bound maintainer
review, and optional byte-archive state. A reviewer-dated receipt identifies an
accountable reviewer, cites an auditable review-system record, and binds the
title, producer, date, version, observations, capability claims, and bounded
requirement claims checked against the primary publisher record.
It is not an independent replication, byte-archive attestation, or target-
population validation. `pending archive` means
that no repository-controlled byte snapshot and SHA-256 are recorded; ordinary
informative references do not require one. Claims that depend on immutable
source bytes, including `triangulated_empirical`, do. The separation rules are
stated in [TRACE-001](requirements.md#traceability-rules). Whole-standard
publication is gated separately by `npm run release:check` on a clean exact
commit.

## Catalog maintenance

The repository's machine-readable
[`source-evidence-manifest.json`](source-evidence-manifest.json), validated by
[`source-evidence-manifest.schema.json`](../schemas/source-evidence-manifest.schema.json),
binds requirement IDs to source records. The normative catalog-maintenance rules
are owned by [`TRACE-001`](requirements.md#traceability-rules). In practical
terms, publication requires each relied-on row to have a current, projection-
bound maintainer review receipt, retrieval date, evidence class, supported
requirement IDs, and explicit support strength. Byte-level artifact paths,
lengths, and SHA-256 are additionally required when the claim depends on
archived bytes.
An
empirical claim cites the stable structured observation required by `TRACE-001`,
including its population and sampling, method, result, uncertainty and selection
conditions, limits, and data or benchmark lineage. Capability support and
target-population validation remain separate; adjacent-population observations
do not clear a repository-SDLC validation gap. Producer, funding, archive, and
common-mode relationships used for triangulation are machine-checked. Contrary
evidence and population limits remain visible, and a later source update gets a
new catalog record rather than silently replacing the prior record.
